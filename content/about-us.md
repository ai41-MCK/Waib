### About Us

- Image: `scraped/assets/shopfront-2633ceea.jpg` — alt: ""

Millbrook Home & Garden was started in 1987 by Peter and Margaret Hartley in a small shop on Brook Street. What started as a hardware store soon grew into the home and garden shop our customers know and love today.

Today the shop is run by their daughter Anne and a team of 12 friendly staff. We still believe in the same things as on our first day: good quality products, fair prices and friendly service.

We choose every product in our range ourselves, and many of them are made by small makers from across the country. If you can't find what you are looking for, just ask us - we are always happy to help!

In 2003 we opened our first online shop and in 2009 we launched this new website, so customers from all over the country can now shop with us.
